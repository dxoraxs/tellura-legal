# Tellura Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

## Who we are

Tellura is an audio guide app for walking around a city. If you have a question about your data, write to vlntn.nzrnko@gmail.com.

## What we collect

### Your account

The first time you open Tellura, the app creates a guest account for you. It is a random id, not tied to your name, email or phone number. We use it to keep your settings, saved places, ratings and listened places on our server. After you sign in, your saved places, ratings and listened places come back when you reinstall the app or sign in on a new phone; what you did as a guest on that phone is added to your account. You cannot use the app without it.

### Sign in with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google (on iPhone, Apple sign-in will be added later), Google shares your email address and name with us, and we link them to your account. Signing in is optional: everything in the app works as a guest.

### Your settings and activity

We store what you do in the app that you would expect to find again: your interface and narration language, your interests, places you saved, places you listened to and how far, your ratings of places and walks. This is linked to your account.

### Feedback you send

If you send feedback from the app, we store your message, the topics you picked, the app version, the platform (Android or iPhone), the app language and how many walks you finished. It is linked to your account so we can tell repeat feedback apart. Sending feedback is optional.

### Location

Tellura asks for your location to show where you are on the map and which places are near you. This happens on your phone.

Your location leaves the phone only when you ask for walking directions to a place. Then the app sends your current position and the place to our server, which asks Mapbox for a walking route and returns it. We do not store your position. Location is optional: if you do not allow it, the app still works, without the "you are here" dot and directions from where you stand.

### Voice commands

Voice commands are optional. When you use one, your phone's own speech recognizer turns your speech into text. On Android this is usually a Google service, on iPhone it is Apple's. Your voice recording never reaches our server.

The app then sends the recognized text, the language and the recognizer's confidence to our server, which works out the command (for example "what next" or "something nearby") and sends it back. We do not store the text.

### Usage statistics

The app records what happens in it so we can see what works: for example, that the app was opened, a place was opened, a story was played and how much of it was heard, a walk was started or finished. Each event carries your account id, a random id for the current app session, the app version, the interface and narration language, and the country code from your phone's region setting (for example "GE"). The country code comes from your phone's settings, not from your location or IP address.

These events never contain your email, your name, your location, anything you said, or what you typed. They are stored on our server and deleted with your account.

### Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry so we can fix it. A report contains the error, your phone model and system version, the app version, the app session id, the interface and narration language, and whether you use a guest or a signed in account. It does not contain your account id, email, name or location, and the app never sends screenshots or screen recordings.

### Map

To draw the map, the app downloads map images from Mapbox. Like any website, Mapbox sees your IP address and which part of the map you are looking at.

### On your phone only

Downloaded walks and audio, and some app settings, are kept on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show ads and do not share your data with advertisers.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not record or store your voice.
- We do not store your location, and usage statistics never contain it.

All data between the app and our servers travels encrypted (HTTPS).

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores your account and data and runs our server functions. [Privacy policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Mapbox** provides map images and walking routes. [Privacy policy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy)
- **Sentry** receives crash reports. [Privacy policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)
- **Your phone's speech recognizer** (Google on most Android phones, Apple on iPhone) turns speech into text for voice commands. [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Google** signs you in if you choose Google sign-in, and shows the in-app rating prompt from Google Play. [Privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## How long we keep data

We keep your account and everything linked to it until you delete the account. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all data linked to it in the app at any time. See [how to delete your account](delete-account).

## Children

Tellura is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
