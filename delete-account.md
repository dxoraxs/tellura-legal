# Delete your Tellura account

## In the app

1. Open the **Me** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account and data**.
3. Tap **Delete** to confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, and the app starts over with a new, empty guest account.

## Without the app

If you no longer have the app and you signed in with Google, write to vlntn.nzrnko@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Tellura account", from the email address you signed in with. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

A guest account (if you never signed in) is not linked to your name or email, so without the app we cannot tell which guest account was yours.

## What is deleted

Your account and everything linked to it on our server:

- your email and name, if you signed in
- your settings and interests
- saved places, places you listened to and your progress
- your ratings of places and walks
- feedback you sent
- usage statistics recorded for your account

The app also clears this data on your phone. Downloaded walks stay on the phone until you remove the app.

## What is kept

Crash reports, which do not contain your account id, stay with Sentry until they expire, up to 90 days.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy).
